Official website of Western Model United Nations (WEST MUN 2027), an academic student diplomacy conference in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia. About Western Model United Nations (WEST MUN Riyadh)

WEST MUN is an academically rigorous Model United Nations simulation in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia. Our educational mission empowers delegates to deconstruct complex geopolitical deadlocks, author binding resolutions, and build cross-cultural coalitions through consensus-driven parliamentary debate.

  • Official Name: Western Model United Nations (WEST MUN)
  • Conference Edition: WEST MUN 2027
  • Host City: Riyadh, Kingdom of Saudi Arabia
  • Format: Student Diplomacy Conference (In-Person)
  • Eligibility: High School and University Students (Single Delegates and School Delegations)
  • Language: English (UN Parliamentary Procedure)

Committees & Academic Organs

Simulating the United Nations General Assembly, Security Council, Economic and Social Council (ECOSOC), Regional Bodies, and dynamic Crisis Committees.
